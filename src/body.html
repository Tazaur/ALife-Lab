Display

Watch from another device

Host from this device and watch from a phone or another computer. The islands keep running here and the other device only draws them, a little behind real time. It works across the internet too. Keep this tab open while hosting.

Code

On the other device, scan the QR code, open the link, or type the code here.

Sim server

Frames are held back a little and played at an even pace. Smooth keeps a bigger backlog for shaky Wi-Fi; Live has the least delay.

Islands

Each island is a separate world on its own CPU core. The others run unseen at full speed, and every few thousand ticks a few of an island's most successful yips migrate to another. Migration timing is under World. Saves cover every island; the inspector covers the one you are watching.

Running

Saves

Saves hold every island: all yips, brains, genes, resources and settings, compressed and kept in this browser's database. Autosave keeps the latest run safe. Files also load older single-world snapshots.

Yip library

Keep your best yips between runs: their genes, traits and everything their brains learned. A fresh world bred from the library starts with one exact copy of each checked yip, and the rest of the 300 founders are their mutated offspring.
